# Quick Start - AI Dog Image Generation

Your AI image generation is now fully configured and ready to use!

## What's New

You can now generate custom AI images of your dogs when adopting them!

## How to Use

1. **Start the app**: `npm run dev`

2. **Go to Pet Collection**: Navigate to "My Pet Collection" page

3. **Click "Adopt New Pet"**

4. **Enter pet name**: Type a name for your dog

5. **Enable AI Generation**: Check the box "Generate AI Image of My Dog"

6. **Customize description**:
   - A description is auto-filled based on the breed you select
   - You can edit it to customize your dog's appearance
   - Example: "with long golden fur and bright blue eyes, sitting in a flower field"

7. **Generate Preview**: Click "Generate Preview" button
   - Wait 5-10 seconds for AI to create your image
   - Preview the generated image

8. **Regenerate (optional)**: If you don't like it, click "Regenerate Image"

9. **Adopt**: Click "Adopt" to complete with your custom AI image

## Features

- ✨ AI-generated unique images for each dog
- 🎨 Customizable descriptions
- 🔄 Unlimited regeneration attempts
- 💾 Images automatically saved to your pet profile
- 📱 Works on desktop and mobile
- 🎯 Fallback to emoji if generation fails

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific**: Include colors, poses, settings, emotions
2. **Use descriptive words**: "fluffy", "majestic", "playful", "fierce"
3. **Mention the setting**: "in a forest", "on a beach", "in snow"
4. **Add lighting**: "golden hour", "moonlight", "bright sunny day"
5. **Describe expression**: "happy", "calm", "alert", "friendly"

## Cost

- Each image generation costs approximately $0.002
- Images are compressed and stored efficiently
- Extremely affordable for unlimited creativity!

## Troubleshooting

**If generation fails:**
- The app will notify you with an error message
- You can try again with a different description
- You can still adopt without AI image (uses emoji)

**If images don't show:**
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Images might take a moment to load
